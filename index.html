Meds & Labs privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Who we are

Meds & Labs is an app for medicine reminders and blood test results, made by an individual developer.

What the app stores

  • Your medicines, their schedules and your dose history.
  • Your blood test results, the markers you track and their ranges.
  • Your settings, and your sex and birth year if you choose to give them.

All of it is stored only in the app's database on your phone.

What we receive

Nothing. The app has no servers and no account. It has no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. The Android app does not even request the internet permission.

When data leaves the phone

Only when you do it yourself:

  • An encrypted backup file, saved where you choose. It is protected by your password; we can't recover it for you.
  • A CSV export of your results.
  • The Next tests list you share. It holds test names only, no values.
  • A chart you share as a PDF (made on the phone).
  • A lab report you send to an AI chat of your choice. That service's own privacy policy applies. The prompt the app gives you asks it to leave out your name and ID numbers.

Your phone's own backup (for example Google backup on Android) may also copy the app's data to your account, if you turned it on.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms and starting after a reboot: used only for medicine reminders.
  • Files are opened and saved through the system file picker; the app has no access to your other files.

Medical disclaimer

  • Your data stays on this phone. We have no servers and never receive your medicines or test results. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you export a backup or share a file with an AI service yourself.
  • For information only. This app does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Reference ranges are general and may differ from your lab's.
  • Talk to your doctor before changing any medication or acting on a result.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Backup files you saved are yours to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, this page and its date are updated, and the app shows its welcome screen again.
